2. Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible to apply as a driver for Community Transports, you must meet all of the following requirements:

  • Be at least 21 years of age
  • Possess a valid driver's license
  • Own or have regular access to a 4-door car, van, or SUV that is less than 15 years old
  • Have your vehicle registered in the state of Texas
  • Be able to service at least one of the designated Houston-area school districts

Failure to meet any of these requirements will result in your application being marked as "Not Eligible" and you will not be able to proceed with the onboarding process at this time.
